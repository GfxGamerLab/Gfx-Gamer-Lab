Privacy Policy for GFX Screen Recorder

Last updated: October 23, 2026

GFX Gamer Lab built the GFX Screen Recorder app as a free utility tool. This service is provided by GFX Gamer Lab at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

Our app respects your privacy. GFX Screen Recorder does not collect, store, upload, or share any personal data or user information. All screen recordings, audio files, and screenshots captured using the app are saved locally only on your device's internal storage.

Permissions Required

The app requests specific permissions solely to perform core features:

  • MediaProjection: Required to capture screen content for recording.
  • Record Audio: Required if you choose to record microphone or internal game audio.
  • System Alert Window (Overlay): Required to show the floating magic control button over other apps.

None of these permissions are used to track you or steal your personal files.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at our official Telegram or GitHub profile.
